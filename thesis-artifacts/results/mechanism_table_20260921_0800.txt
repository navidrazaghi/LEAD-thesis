3. THE CONTRAST
==============================================================================
   gated   ['rung3', 'rung4']: mean |shift| 0.1564
   ungated ['rung1', 'rung2', 'rung2a', 'rung2b']: mean |shift| 0.0066
   ratio: the gated models reallocate 24x more

   The ungated models are deformable too, so the operator is not what
   reallocates attention -- they simply have no modality axis to
   shift. That is what makes this attributable to the gate rather
   than to the backbone or to the degradation curriculum.

   Note what this does NOT settle: every model carrying the gate also
   trained with the degradation curriculum, so the robustness table
   above still confounds the two. rung2a (curriculum, no gate) is the
   row that separates them.
